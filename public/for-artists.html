For Artists

Your work deserves to travel further.

Out of Africa presents African artists to collectors, interior designers and galleries around the world, with your name, your story and your work at the centre.

Apply to Join

How it works

  1. 1

    Express interest

    Tell us about yourself and share a few examples of your work.

  2. 2

    Get to know each other

    We talk through your practice, your available pieces and how you would like to be presented.

  3. 3

    Build your profile

    Your story, photographs and artwork details are prepared and checked with you before anything is published.

  4. 4

    Reach collectors

    Your work is listed alongside other artists, and enquiries and sales are handled through the marketplace.

What you may need to share

  • Your name, country and preferred contact details
  • A short biography and, if you like, an artist statement
  • The art forms and materials you work with
  • Photographs of available works, with titles where you have them
  • Dimensions, materials and your asking price for each piece

Photographing your work

  • Natural, even light, no heavy filters
  • The whole piece in frame, plus close ups of detail
  • A plain background where possible
  • A portrait or studio photograph, if you are comfortable sharing one

Images remain yours. They are used only to present your work.
